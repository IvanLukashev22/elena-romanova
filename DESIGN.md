## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an architectural, editorial voice calibrated for a private psychoanalytic and psychotherapy practice. Rooted in the principles of International Typographic Style (Swiss Style) and independent literary monographs, the interface privileges restraint, structural honesty, and typographical authority over ornamental decoration.

The emotional resonance is deliberate: quiet, discerning, intellectually rigorous, and therapeutic without resorting to sentimental wellness clichés. Interactions are unhurried and grounded. The aesthetic rejects floating SaaS containers, hyper-saturated gradients, and kinetic noise in favor of broad margins, deliberate asymmetry, typographic hierarchy, and razor-sharp hairline delineation.

## Colors

The palette relies on subtle chromatic tension between paper-like mineral light and deep botanical graphite.

- **Main Light Canvas (`#F2F2EF`)**: A warm, desaturated alabaster recalling uncoated archival book stock. Serves as the primary surface background.
- **Primary Ink (`#202522`)**: Deep charcoal graphite with an imperceptible warm-green undertone. Used for primary typography, authoritative headlines, and structural rules.
- **Dark Accent Foundation (`#26352F`)**: Deep forest graphite. Used exclusively for full-bleed tectonic thematic sections, reflective statements, and contemplative interludes.
- **Secondary Ink (`#747A75`)**: Muted slate-olive. Reserved for metadata, bibliographic citations, caption roles, running headers, and secondary body details.
- **Muted Sage Accent (`#B7C8BC`)**: Soft lichen wash. Utilized sparingly for quiet visual emphasis, selection markers, active states, and editorial highlights.
- **Hairline Dividers (`#E1E3DF` and `#D4D8D2`)**: Precise structural delimiters replacing volumetric shadows. `#E1E3DF` handles internal grid demarcations; `#D4D8D2` establishes section-level boundaries and keyline thresholds.

## Typography

Typography functions as the structural scaffolding of the interface. The pairing pits the literary classicism of **EB Garamond** (for display, pull-quotes, and thematic reflections) against the crystalline, rational geometry of **Manrope** (for structural body, tabular records, clinical specifications, and navigation). Both typefaces feature native, mature Cyrillic support with authentic character shapes.

- Headings employ italicized Garamond sparingly for psychological emphases or conceptual terms.
- All uppercase label styles must adhere to tracked spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mirror archival editorial cataloging.
- Body text preserves an open, breathable line height (`1.6x` to `1.7x`) to encourage sustained reading without cognitive fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is driven by an asymmetric, column-bound Swiss grid:

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Asymmetric balance where primary reading tracts span 7 columns, offset by a 4-column contextual margin for marginalia, footnotes, and scheduling data. Gutters sit at `2rem`, flanked by an outer margin of `4rem`.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Gutters adjust to `1.5rem` with a `2.5rem` canvas margin.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Gutters compress to `1rem` with a `1.25rem` margin. Marginalia collapses beneath corresponding paragraphs.

Spatial hierarchy relies on wide rhythmic intervals (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`) between programmatic clusters, establishing a calm, uncompressed cadence reminiscent of broadsheet art catalogues.

## Elevation & Depth

This system operates without dropshadows, blurred scrims, or skeuomorphic volumetric layers. Depth is constructed exclusively through:

- **Surface Planarity**: Alternation between the primary unbleached paper ground (`#F2F2EF`) and dark architectural zones (`#26352F`).
- **Hairline Framing**: Structural 1px rules (`#E1E3DF` and `#D4D8D2`) delineate spatial coordinates, column breaks, and component perimeters.
- **Overlap & Alignment**: Text boxes and imagery intersect via precise grid coordinates rather than z-index elevation.
- **Modal Treatment**: Overlays use solid light ground framed by `#202522` or a stark `#26352F` backdrop with zero ambient diffusion.

## Shapes

Shapes are strictly non-rounded (`0px`). Every interactive container, input perimeter, button, and image canvas adheres to sharp right-angled geometry. This absolute linearity reflects architectural drafting, unyielding clinical boundaries, and rigorous mid-century print composition.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#202522` rectangular fill with `#F2F2EF` text. Zero corner radius. Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. Text set in `label-md` uppercase. Hover shifts background to `#26352F` with a 1px border highlight using `#B7C8BC`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Unfilled canvas bordered by a 1px stroke of `#202522`. Text in `#202522`. Hover fills with `#B7C8BC` at 25% opacity while maintaining border density.
- **Editorial Text Link**: Inline body or title text featuring a hairline bottom border positioned 4px beneath the baseline, contracting from right-to-left on hover.

### Chips & Meta Indicators
- Zero radius, enclosed in a 1px hairline border of `#D4D8D2`.
- Text styled in `label-sm` using `#747A75`.
- Active state: Fills with `#B7C8BC` at 30% tint; text darkens to `#202522`.

### Lists & Indexes
- Replaces cards with vertical editorial indices.
- Items are bounded top and bottom by 1px rules (`#E1E3DF`).
- Composition: Monospaced numeric index (e.g., `01`, `02`) in `label-sm`, followed by headline in `headline-sm`, and description in `body-sm`. Hover introduces an effortless background wash of `#B7C8BC` at 15% opacity.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Fields**: Underlined-only style or sharp rectangular frame in 1px `#D4D8D2`. Background transparent on `#F2F2EF`. Padding: `0.75rem 0`. Active focus shifts border to `#202522` with a 1px bottom accent in `#B7C8BC`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Sharp squares and diamond indicators (rotated squares). Bordered in 1px `#202522`. Selected state fills the inner bounding box with `#202522`.

### Cards & Content Containers
- Generic floating rounded cards are strictly prohibited.
- Units are organized as **Editorial Modules**: structural grid sections defined by vertical and horizontal hairlines (`#E1E3DF`). Content sits flush against internal padding (`space-lg`) without elevation shadows.

### Clinical Quote & Marginalia Module
- Distinct component dedicated to psychological insights and monograph excerpts.
- Features asymmetric indent (span 2 to 9 on desktop), rendered in `headline-md` italics (EB Garamond), accompanied by a left-anchored 1px vertical hairline rule in `#B7C8BC`.
